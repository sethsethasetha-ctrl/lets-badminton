Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Let Badminton is designed to help clubs organize players, groups, schedules, and scores. Your privacy matters to us.

Information We Collect

Let Badminton does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Player names, gender, skill level, attendance choices, groups, schedules, and match scores are entered by the person using the app. This information is stored locally on the device so the app can provide its features.

How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used only to create groups, generate match schedules, keep scores, and display club session history within the app.

Data Sharing

Let Badminton does not send your data to our servers or share it with third parties. We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, identifiers, usage data, or financial information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Session data remains on your device until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to your device and operating system settings.

Children's Privacy

Let Badminton does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no information is transmitted to us, we are unable to identify or contact individual users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, please contact the app publisher through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.
